      HOW TO PLAY

      Cricket in one swipe

      Batting

      Hold anywhere on the pitch, drag, and release as the ball reaches the batter. Swipe up for a drive, diagonally to place the ball, sideways for a cut or pull, and down to play behind the wicket. Every direction is available. The longer and faster the swipe, the harder the shot. Tap RUN for one crossing; tap again to attempt another. Turn back returns to the previous crease. A keeper with the ball can run you out while you are short.

      Timing

      The bar above the pitch is the timing needle. Release as it crosses the middle for a perfect contact. The meter beside Pause holds your EARLY, GOOD, PERFECT or LATE result. Perfect contact gives a short vibration on supported devices.

      Shots

      Your swipe direction and ball height select the technique. Use LOFT (Shift) for aggressive aerial shots or DEFENSE (D) for softer, safer contact. Tap the active mode again to return to normal. Space plays straight.

      Bowling

      Pick a delivery, set the line and the pace, and choose a field. Extreme pace can send you over the line for a no ball.

      Manual fielding

      In Match setup choose Manual fielding. After the batter hits, select a numbered player on the live map and tap a destination. Chase ball (F) follows the interception. Once collected, throw to the keeper (Q) or bowler (E). Keyboard: 1–9 selects a fielder; arrow keys move on the map. Holding the ball gives the batters time to run.

      Running

      Press R to run and press it again to queue another. Press C to turn back and cancel. A run only counts when both batters reach the far crease, and a late run can end in a run out.

      The scorecard

      Open Pause → Scorecard for the full card: batters, bowlers, extras, partnerships and fall of wickets.
